# GTA 6 Reels — channel brief

Faceless English Shorts/Reels about **Grand Theft Auto VI**: news, updates, facts, tips, records and
clearly-labelled fan theories. 15–30 seconds, one idea per reel, fast and punchy.

## Audience
Gamers 15–35 worldwide (US/UK/India/Pakistan heavy). They already know GTA; no basic explanations.

## Formats (rotate, don't repeat the same one two days running)
- **news** — something that actually happened in the last days (update, patch, record, announcement, sales milestone).
- **facts** — surprising, verifiable facts about the game, its development, map, cities or numbers.
- **tips** — practical advice for players (money, missions, driving, settings). Only tips confirmed by real sources.
- **theory** — fan theories and community discoveries. ALWAYS say "fans think" / "theory" — never present as fact.
- **comparison** — GTA 6 vs GTA 5, Vice City then vs now, Leonida vs real Florida, numbers side by side.

## Accuracy rules (non-negotiable)
- Every number, date and claim must come from the research notes. No invented leaks, no fake insider info, no made-up stats.
- If the research doesn't support a claim, leave it out. Speculation is labelled as speculation.
- Never claim the channel is official or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

## Copyright-safe rules (non-negotiable)
- No Rockstar footage, trailers, screenshots, artwork, logos, music or radio songs. Ever.
- Visual prompts describe GENERIC scenes only: a tropical neon coastal city, a generic unbranded sports car,
  palm trees, a police chase at night, a beach boulevard, money stacks, a gaming setup. Do NOT name or
  describe GTA characters (Lucia, Jason, etc.), the GTA logo, its loading-screen art style, or real brands.
- Names like "GTA 6", "Vice City" and "Leonida" may be SPOKEN and written in text, because we are talking
  about the game. They must never appear as logos or imitate the game's branding.
- No spoilers of story endings or big plot reveals.
- No cheats, glitches for online money, mods or piracy.

## Voice & style
- Narrator: energetic gaming-news voice, short sentences, no filler, no greetings.
- First line hits in under 2 seconds with the most surprising part.
- Last scene ends on a strong line; the renderer adds a "Follow for daily GTA 6 news" card.
- No "like and subscribe" in the script.
